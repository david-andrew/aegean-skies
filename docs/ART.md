# Art direction

Aegean Skies aims for the painted, sunlit Mediterranean of 1920s-30s adventure films: a low-poly
world whose light and color do the work. Everything here is drawn by our own shaders (no
tone-mapping from the engine), so the palette below is what reaches the screen.

## The sea

- Open water is a **luminous teal-blue**, not navy: deep cobalt only straight down in deep water,
  brightening toward the horizon where it takes the sky's color.
- Over sand it turns **turquoise**, glowing from within, and at the shore it is clear enough to
  see the white sand and rocks through it.
- The surface carries **short painted dashes**: pale streaks along the swell, scattered and
  drifting, rather than realistic ripples. Far off it lies flat and calm.
- The sun's glitter is a small, hard sparkle; foam is a lace at the shoreline and a white
  wake behind a hull.

## The sky and its clouds

- Clear cerulean overhead, a pale luminous band along the horizon.
- **Cumulus are huge** and heaped: crisp cream-white sunlit tops, **slate blue-grey shaded
  sides** with a clear painted edge between the two, flat darker bases. Towering ones near
  islands, scattered small ones over open sea, flat stratus sheets at times.
- Sunset: gold and amber light, clouds with dark slate undersides against a glowing sky.
- Night: deep navy, stars, lamps in harbors.

## The islands

- **White-grey limestone** with strong horizontal **strata** and overhangs; cliffs fall straight
  to the sea on some islands, and hidden coves have small white beaches.
- Above the cliffs, **dry ochre/straw summer grass**, grey-green maquis patches and darker pine
  stands. Slope, elevation and geographic aspect distinguish exposed dry slopes from cooler
  wooded folds; pale limestone appears in broad outcrops, not thin bright erosion scars. Dark
  **cypresses** stand up like brush strokes around villas and churches.
- Villages: whitewashed and ochre houses, **terracotta roofs**, stone quays, small boats.
- A small hotel on its own islet, with a dock, cypresses and a garden: the kind of place
  pilots gather in the evening.

## Aircraft

- Bright, saturated liveries (red, cream, navy, olive) with flags and numbers; seaplanes with
  floats or flying-boat hulls, propellers that blur, a white wake on takeoff.
- The sky is often busy: other pilots on their errands, formations, the odd pirate.

## Light

- A soft two-tone (cel-like) light: lit and shaded sides with a narrow soft edge. Whitewash
  stays **warm cream in sun and pale blue-grey in shade**, with broad neutral ambient fill.
  Richer cool shadows belong to foliage/cloud masses, not a blue dye over every wall.
- Distance fades to a pale blue haze, warm gold in the evening.
