# Credits

Almost everything in Aegean Skies is made by the game's own code: the world, the aircraft, the
music and nearly every sound are drawn, modelled or synthesized. The exceptions are listed here.

## Recorded ambience

Four ambience beds are recordings (`assets/ambience/`, played by `src/ambience_beds.jo`). Each was
edited for the game: trimmed to its steadiest 30 seconds, high-passed, layered where two are named,
looped with a 2.5 s crossfade, levelled and re-encoded as 24 kHz Ogg Vorbis. The raw recordings are
not in the repository. The beds are here as part of the game, not as sound files for reuse on their
own.

| Bed | Heard | Made from | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `harbour.ogg` | a busy quay, square or market | "130720_Trogir_17Gradska_4824.wav" by blaukreuz ([freesound.org/s/195725](https://freesound.org/s/195725/)) and "120803_Brela_AT_Wharf_1_F_4824.wav" by blaukreuz ([freesound.org/s/162696](https://freesound.org/s/162696/)) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `village.ogg` | a few people about a square, a café terrace | "People Chatting in the Square of Scorrano, Puglia, Italy" by bassimat ([freesound.org/s/861594](https://freesound.org/s/861594/)) | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| `bar.ogg` | a café or bar room | recordings of bar chatter and a restaurant, provided by the developer | free for use in the game |
| `river.ogg` | the bank of a river or canal | a recording of a river, provided by the developer | free for use in the game |
