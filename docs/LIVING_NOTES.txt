Aegean Skies living-world implementation: chunks 1–12
2026-11-10; coordinator owns chunks 13–14.

Entry point and ownership
Import src/living_world.jo, with schema/enums from src/living_types.jo.
Every mutator borrows World. Keep projections briefly; do not retain a World copy
in scene/UI state. The facade contains exact public function signatures.
The epoch is day0 09:30, 120 ticks/game minute (60 physical ticks/second).
Use tick(world, physical_ticks), or advance_minutes for headless runs.
Money is integer lepta (100 to the phoenix); inventories are milli-packages, fuel is millilitres.
A fuel package is 10L. Hull/engine/throttle and pressure use 0..1000.

Scene integration contract
build_world(seed, harbors) accepts six HarborGeometry entries indexed by place;
empty input computes graphics-free default docks and safe water/beach anchors.
No living module imports application/graphics/audio code.
airborne_trips/scene_requests returns stable trip/person/craft identity, origin and
destination places and dock indices/positions, reason, kind, model/livery indices,
pose, ownership, revision, route/segment progress, encounter/incident and conditions.
Model indices default to the logical Airframe index; coordinator maps to loaded models.
Abstract movement skips a craft whose move_owner is .scene. The coordinator owns
LOD handoff and integrator state: set ownership once, synchronize common POD state,
then commit scene motion with the current tick/revision before arrival/encounter.
commit_motion returns a new revision or -1, rejects stale/repeated writes and
fuel/health increases, and accounts actual scene fuel once. Supply its new revision
to commit_arrival/commit_encounter. Arrival validates safe dock distance and speed;
encounters require current motion commits for both scene-owned participants.
Physical encounters preserve supplied scene damage; no second abstract damage roll.
Scene observed incidents must be marked resolved via commit_motion to avoid reroll.
Take/deliver commands are location checked. Player taking a job loads real cargo but
does not auto-depart: call begin_trip on actual departure. Replay-safe PlayerCommand
receipts are available for UI command handling. The coordinator owns walking/boarding.
Before save, synchronize common state and fold scene ownership. The headless save
rejects live scene ownership; integrator extension/sync is chunk13 work.
The abstract 10-minute final approach and shared Flight coefficients still need
watched-route physical calibration; the headless tests do not claim that gate.

Simplest-correct fixes and explicit model extensions
* Fuel goods accounting uses 1/10000-package ledger units, retaining exact ml burn
  while player/market inventory stays milli-packages. Residual pennies are retained.
* Default harbors match coast_point/quay construction without importing objects.jo;
  conservative boat routes go outside islands and return public boats to origin.
* Pirate sortie geometry uses a 250m cove climb, closest reachable point on the
  announced lane and a small bounded orbit. The report's higher climb/midpoint
  search could require more than the available 30L before reserve/return.
* Essential legal imports buy fuel, medicine and parts before discretionary stock.
  Documented market working capital is granted at actual need, at most once/day.
* Thirty-day sweeps exposed parked couriers with no fuel cash. Actual resolved
  outside shipments now create funded local supply reports/missed-shipment notices
  for Yannis at Porto. Source business funds escrow and supplies real reserved fuel
  in kind, enough outbound/home/reserve; no advance cash reward or invented stock.
  Unused charter reservations are released; refunded reward returns to its business.
  Fuel taken without attempting the job creates noninterest restitution to supplier.
  These causal business receipts are an explicit extension beyond the initial jobs.
* Outside pirate credit is a genuine external liability with an explicit creditor
  account/boundary flow; repayments remit it outside. Existing crew cash is used first.
* Recovery services and emergency care are paid and keep the same person/aircraft IDs;
  no silent repair, refuel, duplication or teleport. Forced closure/broken plane/absent
  mechanic fixtures begin a funded recovery within48h. Natural sweeps need no subsidy.
* Pruning retains eight-day terminal history plus live ownership/debt/bounty/aftermath
  dependencies. Canonical maps are sorted, including struct-keyed maps; IDs never reset.
* Schema1 typed added-field defaults are supported. Other schema/terrain versions are
  rejected without overwrite; corrupt newest slot falls back to the valid older one.
